Section 2
18 Articles of Faith
Division II, Section A. Recite these for the oral exam. Tap any group to show all articles.
GROUP 1 · Articles 1–5 · WHO GOD IS

Theme: GOD-3-R-D-D (God is One, Three Persons, Redeemer, Died/Rose, Door)

1
We believe in One true and living God, the Creator of heaven and earth, and the Father of the Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.
2
We believe there are three persons in the Godhead; namely: the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost.
3
We believe that Jesus Christ is the Redeemer of mankind.
4
We believe that Jesus died, was buried, arose, and ascended into heaven.
5
We believe Jesus Christ to be the door to Eternal Life, and we are to enter into the City through and by Jesus Christ.
"The ONE God in THREE persons REDEEMS us — He DIED and ROSE and is the DOOR."
GROUP 2 · Articles 6–8 · HOW TO ENTER

Theme: THIEF-3-FATHER (No shortcut, Three sacraments, Brotherhood)

6
We believe if any attempt is made to enter the fold of God without following Christ's instructions, we make a complete failure and cometh before the Mighty One as a thief and a robber.
7
We believe there are three sacraments ordained of Christ in the New Covenant, namely: Baptism, the Lord's Supper, and the Washing of Feet.
8
We believe in the Fatherhood of God and the Brotherhood of man.
"No thief enters — the THREE sacraments open the door under ONE Father." Articles 6-7-8 = Access requires the right path and the right rites.
GROUP 3 · Articles 9–11 · HOW WE LIVE

Theme: ONE-GOLDEN-FREE (One in Christ, Golden Rule, Free and Equal)

9
We believe all are one in Christ, who does His will.
10
We believe we should do unto all men as we would have them do unto us.
11
We believe all men are born free and equal.
"One Body, Golden Rule, Free and Equal." These 3 are the social gospel — how Christians treat each other and the world.
GROUP 4 · Articles 12–18 · THE BIBLE

Theme: BIBLE-EVERY-GENESIS-GOSPELS-ACTS-REVELATION-PROFIT

12
We believe that the Holy Bible is the book which points out the Way of Eternal Life through obedience.
13
We believe to be saved we must live by every word (Pertaining to salvation) that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.
14
We believe the Bible from Genesis to Malachi to be a Jewish guide foreshadowing the coming of Christ.
15
We believe the Gospel recorded in Matthew, Mark, Luke and John contains the Plan of Salvation.
16
We further believe the Book of Acts may be used as by-laws, in part, and as History of the Apostolic work.
17
We believe the Book of Revelation was sent from God signified by an angel unto John for inspiration and encouragement of the disciples of Christ.
18
We believe the scripture is Profitable for Doctrine, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness. The man of God may be perfect, thoroughly furnished unto all good works.
B-E-G-G-A-R-P — Bible, Every Word, Genesis-Malachi, Gospels, Acts, Revelation, Profitable. "A BEGGAR for scripture — the man of God profits from every word."

Master Chain — 4 Groups

GOD ENTERS, WE LIVE, THE BIBLE LEADS

4 words · 4 groups · 18 articles. Rehearse the chain, then fill in the articles within each group.

ORAL EXAM STRATEGY: State "The Church of the Living God has 18 Articles of Faith." Then recite by group. This shows you know both the number AND the content.
Section 3
Plan of Salvation
Division II, Section B. Know at least two scriptures for the written exam.

What COTLG Teaches

Salvation is available to all through:

1
Repentance
Turning away from sin
2
Faith
Believing in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior
3
Baptism
In water, by immersion
4
Receiving the Holy Ghost
The gift of the Holy Spirit
5
Holy Living
Living a holy and sanctified life

Acts 2:38 (KJV) Must Know

Acts 2:38 Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.

WHY: The foundational "Plan of Salvation" verse. Contains all four steps: Repent, Baptized, Remission, Holy Ghost.

Acts 2:38 = R-B-R-H: Repent, Be baptized, Remission, Holy Ghost. "Peter's 4-step sermon."

John 3:5 (KJV) Must Know

John 3:5 Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

WHY: Confirms the necessity of water baptism AND the Spirit. Jesus himself says it — strong doctrinal anchor.

John 3:5 = BORN AGAIN needs WATER + SPIRIT. "Double-born, double sure."

Romans 10:9–10 (KJV)

Romans 10:9–10 That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.

WHY: Shows faith + confession as elements of salvation.

For the written exam, cite the scripture reference AND the point it proves. Example: "Acts 2:38 proves that baptism is required for remission of sins."
Section 4
Plan of Salvation & Ordinances
Pages 10–17. Know how to walk a new member through salvation AND explain all three ordinances.

Receiving a New Member — 6 Steps

G-R-C-B-H-W: Gospel, Repent, Confess, Baptize, Holy Ghost, Welcome. "God's Recipe Creates Beautiful Holy Witnesses."
1
Present the Gospel
God loves them. All have sinned (Rom 3:23). The wages of sin is death (Rom 6:23).
2
Call to Repentance
Acts 2:38 — "Repent, and be baptized every one of you..." Ask them to confess their sins and turn to God.
3
Confession of Faith
Romans 10:9-10 — Confess with the mouth, believe in the heart. Lead them in a prayer of salvation.
4
Water Baptism
Baptism by immersion — in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost (Matt 28:19). Symbolizes death, burial, resurrection (Rom 6:4).
5
Receive the Holy Ghost
Acts 2:38 — "...ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost." Pray with them for the filling of the Holy Spirit.
6
Welcome into the Body
Welcome them as a new member of COTLG. Encourage attendance, discipleship, and participation in the Ordinances.

Ordinance 1 — Water Baptism

WHAT: Full immersion in water in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.

Matthew 28:19Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.
Romans 6:3–4Know ye not, that so many of us as were baptized into Jesus Christ were baptized into his death? Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.
BAPTISM = BURIAL + RESURRECTION. Down = buried with Christ. Up = raised with Christ. Matt 28:19 = COMMAND · Romans 6:4 = MEANING.

Ordinance 2 — The Lord's Supper

WHAT: Bread (representing Christ's body) and Water (representing His blood).

1 Corinthians 11:23–25...the Lord Jesus the same night in which he was betrayed took bread: And when he had given thanks, he brake it, and said, Take, eat: this is my body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me. After the same manner also he took the cup... saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.

Why WATER (not wine)?

The Church uses water as an expression of purity, temperance, and holiness. Water represents the pure and living Word of God, the life Christ gives freely. The Church teaches abstinence from alcohol.

John 4:14But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.
WHY WATER? PURE + FREE. Jesus offered LIVING WATER (John 4:14), not wine. "Pure water for a pure Church."

Ordinance 3 — The Washing of Feet

WHAT: Ceremonial washing of one another's feet, as instituted by Jesus.

John 13:14–15If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you.

WHY: Jesus gave this as a direct command — "ye also ought to." An act of humility, service, and love.

FEET WASHING = HUMILITY + OBEDIENCE. Jesus said "OUGHT to" — not optional. John 13:14-15 is the command. Towel + Basin = tools of the servant-leader.
WRITTEN EXAM: Be ready to name all THREE ordinances in order: (1) Baptism, (2) Lord's Supper, (3) Washing of Feet. Give scripture for each. Show you know WHY, not just WHAT.
Section 5
The Lord's Prayer
Know WHY the Church prays it, quote the key phrase, and identify the seven petitions.

Key Phrase to Memorize

"This prayer sustains all our needs and it contains seven petitions."
Memorize this EXACT phrase for the oral exam.

Why We Pray the Lord's Prayer

Matthew 6:9–13After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

Jesus himself taught this prayer to His disciples, saying "After this manner pray ye." The Church of the Living God follows the direct instruction of Christ.

The Seven Petitions

H-K-W-B-F-L-D — "Heavenly King Wants Bread, Forgiveness, Light, and Deliverance"
H
Hallowed be thy name
Worship / Praise petition
K
Thy kingdom come
Kingdom petition
W
Thy will be done
Submission petition
B
Give us this day our daily bread
Provision petition
F
Forgive us our debts/sins
Forgiveness petition
L
Lead us not into temptation
Guidance petition
D
Deliver us from evil
Deliverance petition

Note: The doxology ("For thine is the kingdom...") is the closing praise — not counted as a petition.

WRITTEN/ORAL: Quote the exact phrase: "This prayer sustains all our needs and it contains seven petitions." Then name them. This shows you know the teaching, not just the prayer.
Section 6
The Role of the Pastor
Division VIII. Understand the pastor's role, Ephesians 4:11–12, and the pastor as shepherd.

Ephesians 4:11–12 (KJV) Key Verse

Ephesians 4:11–12And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ.

Pastors are a gift from God. Their purpose is threefold:

P
Perfect the saints
Equip believers for maturity
M
Ministry work
Service and mission
E
Edify the body
Build up the body of Christ
Eph 4:11–12 = P-M-E: Perfecting saints, Ministry work, Edifying the body. "Pastors Make Everything grow."
Pastors Deserve Financial Support
1 Corinthians 9:13–14Do ye not know that they which minister about holy things live of the things of the temple?... Even so hath the Lord ordained that they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.
1 Timothy 5:17–18Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in the word and doctrine. For the scripture saith... The labourer is worthy of his reward.
1 Cor 9:14 = "Live of the gospel." · 1 Tim 5:17 = "Double honour." Both say: SUPPORT YOUR PASTOR.
The Pastor as Shepherd
John 10:11, 14I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep... I am the good shepherd, and know my sheep, and am known of mine.
1 Peter 5:2–4Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind; Neither as being lords over God's heritage, but being ensamples to the flock. And when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away.

Christ is the Chief Shepherd; the pastor is His under-shepherd.

S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D. Acrostic:
S — Serves the flock
H — Heals the wounded
E — Equips the saints (Eph 4:12)
P — Protects from false teaching
H — Honors God above self
E — Edifies the Body
R — Rules with gentleness
D — Disciples the next generation
ORAL EXAM: Reference John 10:11 and call Jesus the "Good Shepherd." Then say: "The pastor serves as an under-shepherd, accountable to Christ the Chief Shepherd (1 Peter 5:4)." This shows theological depth.

The Ministerial Guide
Division X — know ALL of this for the written exam, including scriptural proofs.

The Call to Ministry

Ministry is a divine calling, not a human appointment.

Romans 10:15And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!
Jeremiah 1:5Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations.
A minister is SENT, not self-appointed. The call is God's sovereign choice.

Qualifications — BASH-GRIP

From 1 Timothy 3:1–7

B
Blameless in character
A
Apt to teach
S
Sober & vigilant
H
Holy in home life
G
Good report / reputation
R
Rules his house well
I
Incorruptible — not greedy
P
Patient & peaceable
BASH-GRIP — "A minister must BASH any disqualifier and have a firm GRIP on righteousness."

Ministerial Conduct & Duties

2 Timothy 2:15Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

Duties: Preach and teach the Word · Administer the three ordinances · Visit the sick · Comfort the bereaved · Counsel the troubled · Lead by example · Submit to Church authority

Titus 1:9Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

Ordination

1 Timothy 4:14Neglect not the gift that is in thee, which was given thee by prophecy, with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery.
Acts 13:2–3As they ministered to the Lord, and fasted, the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them. And when they had fasted and prayed, and laid their hands on them, they sent them away.
ORDINATION = CALL + CHURCH + HANDS. "You cannot ordain yourself — God calls, the Church confirms, the presbytery lays hands."
WRITTEN EXAM: Know 1 Tim 3:1–7, 2 Tim 2:15, 1 Tim 4:14, and Titus 1:9 as Division X anchor scriptures. For each qualification, be able to state the verse reference.
Section 8
Four Key Exam Questions
Likely on both written and oral exams. Know these answers and scriptures.
Q1 — Why do you believe in the Church of the Living God (C.W.F.F.)?
Model Answer
I believe in the Church of the Living God, C.W.F.F., because it was established on the Word of God and the foundational doctrines of Jesus Christ. The Church stands on the full gospel — repentance, baptism, the Holy Ghost, and holy living. It was founded by Chief William Christian in May 1889 in Wrightsville, Arkansas, under the direction of the Holy Spirit. The Church teaches the whole counsel of God and holds fast to the Apostles' doctrine.
1 Timothy 3:15 ⭐ KEY VERSEBut if I tarry long, that thou mayest know how thou oughtest to behave thyself in the house of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.
Matthew 16:18And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.
ANCHOR: 1 Timothy 3:15 — "The Church of the Living God = the pillar and ground of the TRUTH." This verse has our church's NAME in it!
Q2 — Why do you pray as COTLG teaches?
Model Answer
I pray as the Church of the Living God teaches because prayer is the ordained means of communication between God and His people. The Church teaches that we should pray according to the pattern given by Jesus Christ Himself — including the Lord's Prayer, which sustains all our needs and contains seven petitions. We are also taught to pray in faith, in the Spirit, and in obedience to God's Word.
Matthew 6:9After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven...
1 Thessalonians 5:17Pray without ceasing.
James 5:16...The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.
Four Pillars of Prayer: Jesus COMMANDED it (Matt 6:9) · Paul REQUIRED it (1 Thess 5:17) · James PROMISED it (James 5:16) · Paul INSTRUCTED it (Phil 4:6)
Q3 — What are the elements used for the Lord's Supper?
Model Answer
The Church of the Living God uses BREAD and WATER for the Lord's Supper. The bread represents the broken body of Jesus Christ given for our sins. The water represents the shed blood of Jesus Christ for the remission of our sins. The Church uses water rather than wine as a symbol of purity, holiness, and temperance — consistent with the Church's teachings on clean and holy living.
BREAD
Represents the Body of Christ
WATER
Represents His blood — pure, free, living
B + W = Lord's Supper. BREAD for His body, WATER for His blood. "Pure water for a pure Church."
Q4 — What did Christ say concerning Feet Washing after Supper?
Model Answer
After the supper, Jesus rose from the table, laid aside His garments, took a towel and a basin of water, and began to wash His disciples' feet. After He had finished, He said to them: "If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you." (John 13:14-15 KJV). Jesus declared that no servant is greater than his lord, and that knowing these things, blessed are they that do them (John 13:16-17).
John 13:14–15 ⭐ Key VerseIf I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you.
OUGHT = Obligated Under God's Holy Teaching. It's not a suggestion — Jesus said OUGHT. John 13:17: "Happy are ye if ye DO them."
